# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Interstitial, rewarded and banner ads from Unity LevelPlay 9.8.0 with Unity Ads 4.22.0 (adapter 5.17.0), for Godot 4.7 on Android.
- Consent setters for GDPR, US privacy and child-directed treatment.
- Test mode that serves LevelPlay's public demo app instead of your own.
- `app_left` and `ad_overlong` signals for ads that leave the app or never end, Play's install overlay included.
- The network that filled each ad, in `ad_shown` and `banner_shown`.
- LevelPlay's integration test suite, with any app key.
- Every Unity version pinned, including the two Unity asks for by a range.
